About

I'm Yasir — a creator operating across multiple disciplines. I began with code, expanded into machine intelligence, found my voice in cinema, and ground everything in visual design. Each field feeds the others.

My work sits at rare intersections: films driven by algorithmic thinking, interfaces built with cinematic intuition, AI experiments that feel human. I'm not interested in being a specialist; I'm interested in being deeply fluent across the things that matter.

Currently building personal projects in AI-assisted filmmaking, generative design, and next-generation web experiences. Always learning, always shipping.
